Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Draft for business review. This local website preview does not send or save enquiry form contents. The privacy contact, enquiry provider and the business's approved information handling practices must be confirmed before live enquiries are enabled.

About this website

This is the website preview for Build Invest Grow, a mortgage broking and lending business in Caringbah · St George · Sutherland Shire. The business operator is Build Invest Grow Pty Ltd. For its current published privacy policy, visit the company privacy policy. This preview notice describes only the local draft.

Information in the enquiry form

The form asks for your name, phone number, email address, loan interest, preferred next step and an optional message. Please leave identity documents, bank account details and financial documents out of this form.

In this preview, the information is used only in your browser to check the form and display your selected enquiry type. The form does not transmit the information, store it in browser storage or create an appointment.

When live enquiries are enabled

The final policy and collection notice must explain how enquiry information is collected, held, used and disclosed, the confirmed purpose of follow-up, any service providers that receive it and any relevant overseas disclosures.

Any later loan application and supporting financial documents will need their own appropriate information handling process. The website enquiry is not a credit application.

Access, correction and privacy complaints

The final policy must set out how to request access to or correction of personal information, how to contact the business about privacy concerns, and how complaints will be handled. The privacy contact and collection arrangements for the new enquiry service still need confirmation.

Analytics and advertising

This preview has no advertising pixel, Google Analytics or third-party tracking script. Website fonts and images are hosted with the page. The final policy will need to reflect the actual hosting and any measurement tools enabled at launch.

Changes before launch

This draft must be updated to reflect the business's actual practices and approved by the business before publication. The final policy will carry its own update date.
